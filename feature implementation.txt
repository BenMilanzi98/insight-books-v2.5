Feature Summary: Product-Level Tax Management for Inventory & POS
Overview
This feature allows businesses to define their own taxes and assign one or multiple taxes to individual products within the inventory module. Once configured, taxes are automatically applied during POS sales and clearly itemized on receipts, ensuring transparency and compliance.

What the Feature Solves


Supports businesses with multiple tax obligations


Ensures accurate, consistent tax calculation


Produces compliant receipts with full tax breakdown



Core Capabilities
Users can create and manage multiple tax types (percentage or fixed).


Each product can have zero, one, or multiple taxes assigned.


Taxes are applied automatically when the product is sold in POS.


POS users cannot edit taxes; they are controlled from inventory.


All applied taxes are listed clearly on the receipt.




How It Works (High Level)
User defines tax types (e.g. VAT, Levy).


User assigns applicable taxes to each product.


When a product is sold:


Base price is calculated.


All assigned taxes are applied automatically.


POS totals update instantly.


Receipt shows:


Subtotal


Individual tax amounts


Total tax


Grand total



Receipt & Transparency
Each tax is shown by name.


Taxes are grouped in the receipt summary.


Customers can clearly see:


What tax was charged


How much was charged


Improves trust and regulatory compliance.



Business & Reporting Impact
Tax amounts are stored per sale for historical accuracy.


Tax totals can be reported daily, weekly, or monthly.


Revenue and tax amounts are separated for accounting.


Supports VAT reporting and audits.



Permissions & Control
Only Admin or Manager can:


Create taxes


Assign taxes to products


Cashiers can only view applied taxes during sales.



Edge Case Handling
Products without tax remain tax-free.


Multiple taxes stack correctly per product.


Tax changes do not affect past sales

2. Feature Summary: Multi-Tax Accounts & Tax Tracking
Overview
The system will allow users to create multiple tax accounts within the Tax Management module. Each tax collected during sales, invoicing, or payroll will be automatically assigned to its respective tax account, enabling accurate tracking, reporting, and compliance with statutory obligations.

What This Enables
Clear separation of different tax obligations


Accurate tracking of how much tax is collected per tax type


Easy preparation for tax remittance and reporting


Elimination of manual tax reconciliation



Supported Tax Examples
VAT


PAYE


Withholding Tax


Levies (Tourism Levy, Municipal Levy, etc.)


Each tax type is treated as a separate tax account.

Core Capabilities
Users can create multiple tax accounts, each representing a specific tax obligation.


Each tax account has:


Name (e.g. VAT, PAYE)


Rate or calculation rule


Status (active/inactive)


Taxes applied during transactions are automatically routed to the correct tax account.


One product or transaction can contribute to multiple tax accounts simultaneously.



How Tax Assignment Works
User defines tax accounts in the Tax Management module.


Each tax is linked to a specific tax account.


When a transaction occurs:


Tax is calculated.


The calculated amount is posted to the linked tax account.


The system keeps a running balance per tax account.



Tracking & Visibility
Each tax account shows:


Total tax collected


Total tax refunded or adjusted


Net tax payable


Users can view tax totals:


Daily


Monthly


3. Feature Summary: Payment Processing 
Overview
The system will support user-defined payment accounts instead of fixed payment methods.
 Only Cash will exist as a default system account. All other payment accounts (e.g. Bank, Mpamba, Airtel Money, POS Terminal) are created and managed by the client.

Single Source of Truth: Payment Accounts Module
What Lives Here
Creation and management of payment accounts


Each account represents where money is received or stored


Examples:


National Bank Account


Standard Bank POS


Mpamba


Airtel Money


PayChangu


Mobile Wallet


📌 Payment accounts are not hard-coded.

Default System Accounts
Cash (system-created, cannot be deleted)


All other accounts:


Created by the user


Editable and deactivatable
Maintain the hardcoded accounts for existing users 



Payment Account Capabilities
Each payment account includes:
Account name


Account type (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money, Wallet)


Optional reference (account number, wallet ID)


Status (active/inactive)



How Payment Processing Works
User creates payment accounts in Payment Management


POS and Invoicing load active payment accounts


At checkout:


Cashier selects one or more payment accounts


Amounts are allocated per account


System records payment per account automatically



POS & Sales Behavior
Cashiers see only active payment accounts


Supports:


Single-account payments


Split payments across multiple accounts


Cash account always available



Receipts & Transparency
Receipt shows:


Payment breakdown by account


Amount per account


Improves reconciliation and auditing



Reporting & Reconciliation
Daily reports show:


Total collections per payment account


Enables:


Bank reconciliation


Mobile money settlement tracking


Cash vs non-cash analysis



Permissions & Control
Admin / Finance:


Create, edit, deactivate payment accounts


Cashiers:


Select accounts during payment


No account configuration access



Edge Case Handling
Inactive accounts cannot be used for new payments


Historical payments remain linked to original accounts


Cash cannot be deleted or disabled

Vii. Unified Account Codes & Categories Management (Core Accounting Rule)
Problem Statement
Currently, account codes and account names are system-created and locked, while other modules (Expenses, Budgeting) allow users to create free-text categories.
 This leads to:
Duplicate categories (e.g. Meals & Entertainment vs Meals and Entertainment)


Expenses not mapped to any account code


Budget tracking mismatches


Inaccurate financial statements


Broken reporting logic


This breaks a core accounting principle: every transaction must map to an account code.

Proposed Solution
Introduce one central place to manage account codes and account names, and make all financial categories derive from this structure.

Single Source of Truth: Chart of Accounts (Ledger / Advanced Accounting)
What Lives Here
List of default account codes with:


Account code


Account name


Account type (Income, Expense, Asset, Liability, Equity)


System-provided default accounts (read-only)


User-created accounts (editable)


📌 This becomes the only place where account categories are defined.
