/insightbooks Admin Intelligence and Lead CRM — Full Implementation Roadmap

The uploaded specification contains two tightly connected systems:

A SaaS Business Intelligence Command Centre for executives, finance, customer success, technical operations and security.
A complete Lead Management and Sales CRM covering lead capture, qualification, Pipeline, proposals, conversion, onboarding, training and retention.

They should be implemented as one integrated platform inside /insightbooks, sharing users, permissions, Tenants, subscriptions, billing, notifications, Audit Trails and analytics rather than operating as separate disconnected applications.

1. Overall Product Vision

The /insightbooks section must become the central operating system for the company behind InsightBooks.

It must allow management to answer:

How is InsightBooks performing financially?
How many customers are joining, paying, renewing or leaving?
Which products and features generate the most value?
Which customers are healthy, inactive or likely to cancel?
Which leads require immediate follow-up?
How effective is the sales team?
What operational, infrastructure or security risks exist?
What should management do next?

2. Non-Negotiable Architecture Principles
2.1 Platform and Tenant data must remain separate

The Admin system may analyse Tenant activity and manage platform relationships, but it must not silently post or modify Tenant:

Journals
Sales
Purchases
Expenses
Payroll
Inventory
Taxes
Assets
Liabilities
Customer payments
Supplier payments

Any authorised assistance inside a Tenant must use the existing secure support-access or impersonation workflow with complete Audit Trails.

2.2 The removed Chart of Accounts route must remain removed

The following must not be reintroduced:

/insightbooks/chart-of-accounts

The “System chart of accounts” navigation item must remain absent.

Normal Tenant Chart of Accounts functionality remains under the Tenant accounting application.

2.3 Transactional data and analytics data must be separated

Operational records remain the source of truth.

Analytics may use:

Read models
Aggregation tables
Materialised views
Event streams
Daily snapshots
Cached metrics

Analytics must never become an independent competing financial source.

2.4 Platform revenue must not be confused with Tenant revenue

The Admin Dashboard should report InsightBooks SaaS revenue from:

Platform subscriptions
Platform invoices
Platform payments
Add-ons
MRA EIS plans
Setup fees
Usage charges
Credits and refunds

It must not combine these figures with sales made by Tenants to their own customers.

2.5 No duplicated commercial effects

The system must prevent:

Duplicate Leads
Duplicate Tenants
Duplicate Subscriptions
Duplicate platform invoices
Duplicate payments
Duplicate affiliate commissions
Duplicate onboarding projects
Duplicate Lead conversions
Duplicate activities
Duplicate notifications
Duplicate analytics events
2.6 AI must remain evidence-based

AI-generated insights must always show:

The underlying metric
The analysed period
The comparison period
The contributing factors
Confidence or reliability
Data freshness
Links to supporting records

AI must not invent reasons, revenue, customer behaviour or recommendations without supporting evidence.

2.7 Bilingual readiness

Every new page and component must use the existing English–Chichewa internationalisation architecture.

No newly implemented page may be built with hardcoded English-only strings.
