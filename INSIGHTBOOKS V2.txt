
# INSIGHTBOOKS V2

## Accounting Architecture & Financial Reporting Guide

### Version 1.0

**Prepared For:** Insight Innovations Development Team

**Product:** InsightBooks V2

**Objective:** To build an accounting engine that meets international accounting standards, supports audit requirements, and produces lender-ready financial statements for businesses seeking financing.

---

# 1. Vision

InsightBooks V2 is not simply an accounting application.

It is a **Financial Operating System** that enables businesses to:

* Record every financial transaction accurately.
* Produce reliable financial statements.
* Meet audit requirements.
* Demonstrate financial health to banks and investors.
* Generate forecasts that assist management in making financial decisions.

The accounting engine must become the foundation upon which every other module operates.

---

# 2. Core Accounting Philosophy

Every financial transaction must pass through the General Ledger.

Modules such as:

* Sales
* Purchases
* Inventory
* Payroll
* Banking
* Fixed Assets
* Loans

do **not** directly affect financial statements.

Instead, they generate journal entries which are posted to the General Ledger.

Financial statements must only read data from the General Ledger.

---

```
        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼

```

This architecture ensures consistency and prevents modules from creating inconsistent financial data.

# 5. Accounting Rules

The following rules are mandatory.

## Rule 1

Every transaction must balance.

```
Total Debits = Total Credits
```

The system must reject any transaction that does not balance.

---

## Rule 2

Posted transactions cannot be edited.

Corrections must be made using

* Reversal
* Adjustment Journal

---

## Rule 3

Every transaction must have an audit trail.

Record

* User
* Time
* Previous Value
* New Value
* Device
* IP Address

---

## Rule 4

Financial reports must never calculate directly from Sales or Inventory tables.

Reports must only read from the General Ledger.

# 9. Bank Loan Readiness Module

This module is designed to help businesses prepare for loan applications by presenting key financial indicators in one place.

The system should provide:

### Financial Ratios

* Current Ratio
* Quick Ratio
* Gross Profit Margin
* Net Profit Margin
* Debt-to-Equity Ratio
* Interest Coverage Ratio
* Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
* Working Capital

### Business Performance Indicators

* Average Monthly Revenue
* Revenue Growth
* Net Profit Trend
* Cash Flow Stability
* Customer Concentration
* Expense Trends
* Inventory Turnover
* Receivables Collection Period

### Loan Readiness Dashboard

Provide an informational dashboard showing whether records appear complete and financially consistent. It may include:

* Financial statements generated
* Accounting periods closed
* Tax reports available
* Bank reconciliations completed
* Audit trail completeness
* Credit ratios within target ranges

The dashboard should clearly state that it is an internal assessment and **not** a guarantee of loan approval.

# 14. Development Principles

The development team shall adhere to the following principles:

1. **The General Ledger is the single source of truth** for all financial reporting.
2. **Every module posts to the General Ledger through the Accounting Posting Engine.**
3. **No financial statement shall read directly from operational tables.**
4. **Every transaction must maintain double-entry accounting integrity.**
5. **Posted transactions are immutable and corrected only through reversals or adjustments.**
6. **Every financial figure must be traceable to its originating transaction.**
7. **The platform must support future compliance with IFRS for SMEs and local regulatory requirements without requiring architectural changes.**

## Long-Term Vision

The goal for InsightBooks V2 is not only to help businesses manage their books, but to become the financial record that banks, investors, auditors, and regulators can trust. When a business applies for financing, the reports generated by InsightBooks should demonstrate accurate bookkeeping, sound financial management, and readiness for credit assessment. This vision positions InsightBooks as a true financial operating system for African businesses rather than just another accounting application.
