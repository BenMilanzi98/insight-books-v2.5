InsightBooks V2
Equity Management Module
Developer & Product Specification
1. Purpose
The Equity Management Module is responsible for recording, tracking, and reporting all transactions that affect the owners’ interest in the business.
The module should provide a simple interface for business owners while maintaining accurate accounting records that comply with professional accounting standards.
It should support:
Sole Proprietorships
Partnerships
Limited Companies
Startups with Investors
NGOs (where applicable, using accumulated funds instead of owner equity)
The module must integrate seamlessly with the General Ledger, Balance Sheet, Financial Statements, and Reporting Engine.
2. Design Philosophy
Users should never think in terms of journal entries.
Instead, they should answer simple business questions such as:
Who invested in the business?
How much was invested?
Has the owner withdrawn money?
Were dividends declared?
How much profit has been retained?
InsightBooks will automatically generate all accounting entries.

4. Equity Structure
The system should maintain the following equity accounts.
Share Capital
Used by companies with issued shares.
Tracks:
Authorized Capital (optional)
Issued Capital
Paid-Up Capital
Capital Contributions
Records money or assets invested by owners beyond issued share capital.
Supported contribution types:
Cash
Bank Transfer
Mobile Money
Vehicle
Equipment
Furniture
Inventory
Land
Building
Other Assets
The system should automatically update the relevant asset account and the contributor’s capital account.
Additional Paid-In Capital (Share Premium)
Used when shares are issued above their nominal value.
This account is optional and should be available when companies manage equity financing.
Retained Earnings
Stores accumulated profits from previous financial years after year-end closing.
The balance updates automatically during the year-end close.
Current Year Profit
Displays the current year’s profit or loss.
The balance is maintained automatically and transferred to Retained Earnings during the year-end closing process.
Owner Drawings
Tracks money or assets withdrawn by owners for personal use.
Examples:
Cash withdrawals
Asset withdrawals
Personal expense payments by the business
The system automatically reduces equity.
Dividends
Applicable to companies.
Track:
Dividends Declared
Dividends Paid
Outstanding Dividends Payable
The system should distinguish between declaring a dividend (creating a liability) and paying it (settling that liability).
5. Owners & Shareholders
Maintain a register of owners.
For each owner store:
Full Name
Ownership Type
Ownership Percentage
Date Joined
Status
Contact Details
Where applicable, ownership percentages should total 100%. Capital contributions should not automatically change ownership percentages; ownership changes should follow a separate approval process.

Permissions:

View Equity Dashboard

Approve equity transactions

10. User Experience
The module should present business actions rather than accounting concepts.
Example workflow:
Record Owner Investment
Step 1
Select Contributor
Step 2
Select Contribution Type
Step 3
Enter Amount or Asset Value
Step 4
Enter Date
Step 5
Optional Notes
Step 6
Save
The accounting engine automatically:
Updates asset balances.
Updates equity balances.
Posts journal entries.
Updates financial statements.
Updates the owner’s account.
Records the audit trail.
11. Design Principles
The Equity Management Module must:
Keep business workflows simple.
Hide accounting complexity.
Maintain complete accounting accuracy.
Support businesses as they grow.
Produce lender-ready and audit-ready financial reports.
Comply with accepted accounting principles.
Expected Outcome
The Equity Management Module should provide a complete, accurate, and scalable framework for managing owner investments, share capital, retained earnings, dividends, and withdrawals.
It should enable InsightBooks to support businesses from startup through growth and investment while ensuring that every equity transaction is reflected correctly in the Balance Sheet, Statement of Changes in Equity, General Ledger, and other financial reports.
