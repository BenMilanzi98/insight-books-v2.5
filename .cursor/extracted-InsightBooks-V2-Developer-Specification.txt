InsightBooks V2
Developer Specification
Financial Planning & Projection Module
Objective
Design and develop a world-class Financial Planning & Projection Module for InsightBooks V2 that enables businesses to forecast their financial performance, monitor future financial health, and generate lender-ready financial projections. The module must be suitable for SMEs, growing enterprises, banks, auditors, investors, and financial consultants.
This feature should transform InsightBooks from an accounting system into a comprehensive Financial Management Platform.
Design Philosophy
The interface should feel premium, modern, and executive-focused rather than like a traditional accounting package.
Users should immediately understand the financial direction of their business through interactive dashboards, charts, trend analysis, and scenario comparisons.
Every screen should answer one question:
“Where is my business heading financially?”
The UI should emphasize:
Clean layouts
Large KPI cards
Interactive charts
Minimal clutter
Professional financial colors
Responsive design
Smooth animations
Executive dashboard aesthetics
Excellent readability on desktop and tablet

11. AI Forecast Engine
Develop an AI-assisted forecasting engine that analyzes historical accounting data and prepares intelligent projections.
The AI should analyze:
Revenue growth patterns
Expense behaviour
Customer payment trends
Supplier payment patterns
Inventory turnover
Seasonal business cycles
Profitability trends
Cash flow movements
Using these insights, generate a draft forecast that users can review and adjust before saving.
The AI should also produce plain-language summaries, for example:
“Revenue is expected to grow by 16% over the next year based on the previous 24 months of performance.”
“Cash flow is projected to become negative in September due to scheduled loan repayments and seasonal declines in sales.”
“Operating expenses are increasing faster than revenue, which may reduce profit margins unless controlled.”

Expected Outcome
The completed module should allow any business using InsightBooks to:
Understand where the business is heading financially.
Forecast profitability, liquidity, and financial position.
Compare multiple future scenarios.
Monitor actual performance against projections.
Produce lender-ready financial statements and supporting analysis.
Generate professional executive reports suitable for banks, investors, boards of directors, and management.
The module should position InsightBooks as a leading financial planning and business intelligence platform for African SMEs, combining accounting accuracy with strategic forecasting and decision support.
