# Phase 14 Readiness (Loan Readiness Centre)

## Available from Phase 13

- Approved forecast versions + immutable snapshots  
- Projected P&L, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet  
- Revenue / EBITDA / Net Profit / closing & minimum cash  
- Working capital, DSO/DPO/inventory days, cash conversion cycle  
- Debt balances, interest (assumption-based), capex plans  
- Scenario comparison inputs  
- Forecast confidence / historical quality status  
- Assumption disclosures and disclaimers  
- Variance Actual vs Forecast / Budget foundations  

## Not implemented here (Phase 14+)

- Final Loan Readiness Score  
- Lender-specific credit decision rules  
- External credit bureau integrations  
- Guaranteed DSCR / debt-capacity decisions  
- Final executive risk scoring  

## Blockers to confirm per business

- Closed-period history coverage  
- Approved planning configuration  
- Opening BS from verified snapshots (not demo openings)  
- Remaining material historical exceptions from Phases 6–12
