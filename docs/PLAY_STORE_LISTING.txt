InsightBooks: cloud accounting, POS & invoicing for African SMBs.


FULL DESCRIPTION (max 1000 characters)
---------------------------------------
InsightBooks Africa is cloud financial software for SMEs: accounting, sales, and ops in one app.

FEATURES
• Dashboard — KPIs, cash flow, revenue & expenses (per branch when enabled)
• POS — checkout, cart, receipts; Android barcode scan
• Quotations & invoices — create, convert, payments, PDF
• Expenses — categories, approvals, receipts, GL posting
• Payments — cash/bank/wallets, reconciliation
• Reporting — P&L and statements tied to your ledger
• Clients — CRM-lite, AR, statements
• Assets & liabilities — register, depreciation
• Tax — rates, tax accounts, PAYE where configured
• Purchases — suppliers, POs, bills
• GL — chart of accounts, journals, periods, AR/AP
• Budget & forecast (when enabled)
• Payroll / HR runs & reversals (region-dependent)
• Tenants, roles, branches (plan-dependent)
• Android app 

HTTPS, tenant separation. See privacy policy for data and camera (POS). InsightBooks login required.
