# MRA EIS Navigation & Tenant Management Access

**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Status:** Approved for implementation  
**Unlock rule:** Active MRA EIS subscription **OR** entitled status (`ENTITLED_SANDBOX_ONLY` / `ENTITLED_PRODUCTION`)

## Goals

1. System admin: MRA EIS Entitlement is an expandable sidebar dropdown covering all platform EIS sections.
2. System admin: Persistent section strip on every `/insightbooks/mra-eis/**` page.
3. Tenant: Full MRA EIS Centre management nav when unlocked; limited hub when locked.

## Out of scope

Entitlement grant policy changes, PayChangu, public pricing.
